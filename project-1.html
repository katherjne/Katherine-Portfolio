01

Overview

Superpower Mentors runs a mentorship program with measurable results. Their website did not communicate that — and it had to speak to three audiences at once.

Scout paired a ten-person cross-functional team with the client over a single semester. Our brief was a strategic rebrand: sharpen the visual language, restructure the site, and make the case for the program in a way that reaches parents first without alienating prospective mentors or partner schools.

I joined as a junior designer and developer, working across user research, wireframing at every fidelity, and the handoff-ready component system the developers built from.

02

The Client

Trusted online mentorship for youth with ADHD, dyslexia, autism, and other learning differences.

Superpower Mentors matches each mentee with a mentor who is, in the client's words, "the older version of them." The program works — but the proof lived in conversations and testimonials rather than anywhere a visitor would find it.

30% → 0%
Reported reduction in program dropout rates. This number became the anchor of the redesign: everything on the new site had to build toward it.

Research made clear that the three audiences arrive with different questions. We treated them as separate paths through the same site rather than one generic message.

Audience 01
Parents

Arrive asking whether this is safe, credible, and worth their child's time. They need outcomes and reassurance before anything else.

Audience 02
Prospective Mentors

Want to understand the commitment, the matching process, and the kind of impact they can expect to have.

Audience 03
Partner Schools

Evaluate the program as an institutional partner — looking for structure, safeguarding, and evidence.

03

Goals

We set our goals at kickoff and measured every design decision against them.

Goal 01
The Brand

Connect through storytelling to build trust and engagement. Inclusive, but specialized — speaking to a broad audience without losing the specificity that makes the program credible.

Goal 02
The Website

Build a clear path for parents, prospective mentors, and partner schools. Move the site from safe and generic to expressive and hopeful.

04

Process

The semester broke into three phases, each ending in a client review.

Phase 01
Discovery

User research translated into personas and user flows, then a sitemap covering consultation, mentoring, safety, and blog.

Phase 02
Lo-fi Wireframes

Structure before styling — testing the funnel and the six-page architecture while changes were still cheap.

Phase 03
Design System

Color, type, and components codified into a reusable library, then applied across every page at high fidelity.

06

Website

Six pages, one funnel: Home, Mentoring, About Us, Community, Quality, and Blog.

The home page leads with Relate. Empower. Transform. and puts the outcome statistics directly beside it — the proof a parent is looking for, in the first screen. Deep purple #571477 carries the brand, with orange and yellow as accents for emphasis and calls to action.

Below the fold, How It Works walks a visitor through matching, meeting a mentor, and the session itself — the sequence that answers the commitment question for both parents and prospective mentors. The high-fidelity prototypes were delivered handoff-ready, with a component library and documentation for the developer team.
