Hi, I’m Katherine! I’m a second-year student at Northeastern University studying Computer Science and Business Administration with a concentration in Fintech. I’m currently a Dean’s List Scholar in the Khoury College of Computer Sciences, where I’ve maintained a 4.0 GPA.

I’m especially interested in the intersection of technology, business, and user experience. I enjoy working on problems that require both analytical thinking and cross-functional collaboration. At Northeastern, I’m a member of Scout, a student-led design organization where I collaborate with designers, developers, and mentors to build thoughtful, user-centered digital products with real clients.

Outside of Scout, I serve as Merch Chair for Sigma Kappa, managing vendor relationships and coordinating with chapter leadership to bring ideas from concept to execution. My experience in People Operations at ConsumerDirect has further strengthened my interest in how teams, processes, and technology come together to create better products and experiences.

These experiences have shaped my goal of pursuing a career in product management, where I hope to combine my technical and business background with my interests in strategy, collaboration, and building products that solve meaningful problems.
